# Story 3.4: Discount Extraction

## Status

Ready for Review

## Story

**As a** bargain hunter,
**I want** to see how deep a store's discounts go,
**so that** I can pick the best sales.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. The extraction method comes from the store's `extraction_method` field: `shopify_json`, `html`, `structured_data`, or `manual` (FR8).
2. Shopify stores use the collection's product JSON to compute the largest percentage from compare-at and current prices.
3. HTML and structured-data stores read was/now prices and "% off" labels and use the largest percentage found. Was/now price pairs are read first, because a label can come from a banner, and the labels are used only when no price pair is found.
4. Values outside a sane range (below 5% or above 90%) **[Assumption]** are ignored, and the record falls back to "On sale".
5. If nothing can be read, the record is "On sale", not an error.
6. Each method has unit tests using saved page samples (NFR12).
7. The Shopify stores whose sale address is a collection and that answered the Worker in Story 3.2 (`beauty-on-tapp` and `toy-kingdom`) are covered as the first examples, once the first night shows that each one's `/products.json` also answers the Worker. `norman-goodfellows` has a hub page as its sale address and `benji-moon` a single product page, so they use their pages until their addresses are fixed (Story 4.3). Under Armour SA is a custom storefront with no product feed (Story 3.2) and is set to the `html` method. Wellness Warehouse is hidden (blocked).

### The extractors (`packages/core/src/extract/`)

The architecture's tree names `extract/shopify-json.ts`, `html.ts`, `structured-data.ts` and `index.ts`, and its test example calls `extractShopifyDiscount(json)` and expects `50` or `null`. Follow it: **every extractor returns a whole number or `null`**, never throws, and returns only a percentage that is **at least 5 and at most 90** (the range is exported as `MIN_DISCOUNT` and `MAX_DISCOUNT`; anything outside it is ignored, AC4, and "nothing readable" is `null`, AC5). A percentage is `Math.round((was - now) / was * 100)`. [Source: architecture/testing-strategy.md; architecture/unified-project-structure.md]

```typescript
export const MIN_DISCOUNT = 5;   // [Assumption] PRD, criterion 4
export const MAX_DISCOUNT = 90;
export function extractShopifyDiscount(feedJson: string): number | null;
export function extractHtmlDiscount(page: string): number | null;
export function extractStructuredDiscount(page: string): number | null;
export function extractDiscount(method: ExtractionMethod, input: { page: string; feed?: string | null }): number | null;
```

- **`extractShopifyDiscount`** (AC2): `JSON.parse` (a failure gives `null`); for every product and every variant with a numeric `price` and a numeric `compare_at_price` greater than the price (both may be strings such as `"499.00"` or numbers; `null`, `""`, `"0.00"`, text and a compare-at price at or below the price are skipped), the percentage; the **largest** one in range. Products or variants of any shape that are not as expected are skipped without an error. At most 250 products are read (the request asks for 250).
- **`extractHtmlDiscount`** (AC3): scan the page **without scripts, styles, `<nav>`, `<header>` and `<footer>`**.
  1. **Was/now pairs (price-based, the strongest evidence).** For every old-price marker (the was-price patterns of Story 3.3: `old-price`, `standard-price`, `price-before`, `compare-price`, `line-through`, `<s>`, `<del>`, `<strike>`, the text "was R...", and an `aria-label` of the form "Standard Price ZAR a, Sale Price ZAR b"): the **was** amount is the first amount at or after the marker within 300 characters, and the **now** amount is the nearest other amount within 400 characters of the marker that is **smaller** than the was amount; one pair gives one percentage. A pair with no smaller amount is ignored. For the aria-label form both numbers are read from the label.
  2. **Percentage labels** (the badge pattern of Story 3.3: a text element that is only "30% off", "-25%", "save 25%").
  3. **Choice: if any pair gave an in-range percentage, the result is the largest pair percentage; otherwise it is the largest in-range label; otherwise `null`.** The SM's decision: a label can come from a banner, a price pair cannot, so a pair wins even when a label is bigger. **[PO to confirm]**
- **`extractStructuredDiscount`**: the same search over **data in the page** instead of its markup: (a) in the script blocks (and in `ld+json` blocks) a list-price field followed within 200 characters by a price field, for the pairs `listPrice`/`price`, `compare_at_price`/`price`, `originalPrice`/`price`, `wasPrice`/`price` and `oldPrice`/`price` (the key names as in Story 3.3's structured signals; a number or a quoted number); (b) a schema.org `AggregateOffer` with `highPrice` and `lowPrice` (percentage from high to low). The largest in-range percentage, else `null`. If it finds nothing it **falls back to `extractHtmlDiscount`** (AC3 says structured-data stores read both). The page's script text is the one `removeBlocks` already collects.
- **`extractDiscount(method, input)`** (AC1): `shopify_json` uses `extractShopifyDiscount(input.feed)` when a feed text is given and gives a percentage, **else falls back to `extractHtmlDiscount(input.page)`** (a store whose feed does not answer, such as Under Armour SA, still gets a percentage from its page); `html` uses `extractHtmlDiscount`; `structured_data` uses `extractStructuredDiscount`; **`manual` and any unknown method give `null`** (an operator's override supplies the number).

### The pipeline step (`apps/pipeline`)

1. **A module for the feed address.** Move `productsJsonUrl` from `probe.ts` to a new `apps/pipeline/src/shopify.ts` (export it there; `probe.ts` imports it; its tests move with it).

Update `process-store.ts` and the robots tests (update, do not delete). **If the feed path is disallowed, the feed is not requested** and the extraction falls back to the page.
3. **After a `valid` verdict** (and only then), for the store's `extraction_method`:
   - `shopify_json`: request the feed once (`fetchPage`, the same user-agent, timeout, redirect and https rules; read at most 3 MiB with `readLimited`); a non-2xx answer, a timeout, a failed read or a refusal by `robots.txt` means **no feed text** (logged, not an error, **never retried**: a 429 is recorded as `feed_blocked` and not asked again that day);
   - `html` and `structured_data`: the page text already read by `validatePage` (**it must be returned to the caller**, not read twice);
   - call `extractDiscount(method, { page, feed })`.
   An `unverifiable`, `ended` or `blocked` verdict, a non-`ok` class and `manual` stores get **no percentage** and **no feed request**. A throw inside the extraction is caught, logged as `extraction_failed` (`slug`, `err`) and treated as "On sale".
4. **Save it:** the percentage goes into `nextSaleRecord`'s event (`discountPercent`) and into the `run_results` row (`discount_percent`); nothing else about the result row changes.
5. **Log one line `discount_extracted`** with `slug`, `method`, `percent` (a number or null), `feed` (`used`, `fallback`, `blocked`, `not_allowed`, `none`) and `bytes` of the feed read: **numbers and short words only, never page or feed text**. About 54 lines a night.
6. **Politeness, and which stores get a feed request.** The only new request is the feed of a `shopify_json` store, **and only when the path of its `sale_url` is exactly `/collections/<handle>`** (with or without a final slash). That is `beauty-on-tapp` and `toy-kingdom` today (and `under-armour-sa` until the data step below). A hub page (`norman-goodfellows`), a product page (`benji-moon`) and any other path make **no feed request**: the store is read from its page (`feed: none` in the log). At most one feed request per store per run, **never retried** (a 429, a 404 or a timeout is logged and the page is used), and checked against `robots.txt` first with the text already read. **Data step (AC7): `under-armour-sa` is set to the `html` method**, because it has no feed (a request every night would be a 404 every night): `UPDATE retailers SET extraction_method = 'html' WHERE slug = 'under-armour-sa'`, on the **local** database in Task 6 and on the **live** one in Task 8 (a one-row operator update that the PRD's criterion 7 states; the seed never changes the method of an existing store).
