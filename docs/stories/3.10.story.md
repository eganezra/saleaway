# Story 3.10: Fewer False "Ended" Verdicts

## Status

Approved

## Story

**As the** operator,
**I want** the sale-page check to stop calling a store's sale over when the page still shows one,
**so that** the live site does not lose stores that have a sale on.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. A price written with the currency code, for example `ZAR 69.00`, `ZAR69` or `ZAR 1 299.00`, counts as an amount, in the page validator and in the discount extractors.
2. A text element that begins with "Save" and an amount (`Save R 15.00`, `SAVE R15`) counts as a discount signal in the page validator.
3. Words in a page's site furniture, such as "You have no items in your wish list" or "You have no items to compare", do not make the validator call the listing empty. A page that holds discount signals is never called an empty listing.
4. A page that lists priced products but shows no discount signal is called `ended` only if the page does not call itself a sale. If its title, its main heading or the path of its address has one of the words "sale", "special", "deal", "offer", "promo", "promotion", "clearance", "discount", "outlet" or "saving" (also in the plural), the verdict is `unverifiable` (the store stays "On sale"), with a new reason that says the discounts could not be read.
5. Saved samples of the four real pages that were wrongly judged on 2026-10-31 (Spar, Gelmar, Evetech and Mr Price) are part of the page samples, and each gives the verdict listed in "The samples". The existing samples and rules keep their verdicts.
6. The finalize step closes the newest unfinished runs first, so tonight's run is closed even when many older runs are still open (it closed only the 10 oldest on 2026-10-31).
7. After the pipeline is deployed with this change, the next nightly run no longer ends those four stores, and the morning check is done again (README).

### The rules (`packages/core/src/page-scan.ts`, `validate.ts`)

1. **Amounts with a currency code (AC1).** `AMOUNT` and `PRICE` accept `R` **or** `ZAR` before the number, with an optional space (`ZAR 69.00`, `ZAR69`, `ZAR 1 299.00`, `ZAR1,299.00`), still not preceded by a letter or a digit, still `(?![A-Za-z0-9])` after; `amountsWithPositions` reads the number correctly (it removes the letters, spaces and commas: `ZAR 1 299.00` is 1299). The aria-label form of Story 3.4 (`Standard Price ZAR 249.00, Sale Price ZAR 140.00`) already reads `ZAR`: keep it working. The patterns stay linear (a quantifier on a fixed prefix, nothing nested).
2. **"Save R" is a discount signal (AC2).** A text node that **begins** with `save` (any case) and an amount, at most 40 characters long: `Save R 15.00`, `SAVE R15`, `Save ZAR 15.00`. It counts in `signals.percentLabels`? **No: add it to its own signal** `saveLabels` in `ValidationSignals` (a number, logged like the others) and include it in `discountSignals`. (The log line `page_validated` spreads the signals, so the new field appears in it by itself.) It does **not** feed the extractors: a "Save R 15" label gives no percentage, so `extractHtmlDiscount` is unchanged and such a page stays "Up to ... " only if it also has was/now pairs or percentage labels.
3. **Empty listing (AC3).** Rule 7 needs **all** of: fewer than 3 amounts, **no discount signal at all** (`discountSignals === 0`), and a phrase from the list. Remove the plain phrase `no items` from the list (it matches "no items in your wish list" and "no items to compare" and "no items in your cart"); keep `no products`, `0 results`, `nothing found`, `no results`, `sorry, we couldn't find`, `sorry, we could not find`. (With amounts counted correctly Gelmar is rule 5 anyway; the narrower rule protects other stores.)
4. **A page that calls itself a sale (AC4).** Rule 8 stays: 3 or more amounts and no discount signal, page not truncated. Before it returns `ended`, **check whether the page calls itself a sale**: the title, the first `<h1>` or the **path of `expectedUrl`** has one of the words `sale`, `special`, `deal`, `offer`, `promo`, `promotion`, `clearance`, `discount`, `outlet`, `saving`, **whole words, also in the plural**: one pattern, `/\b(?:sale|special|deal|offer|promo|promotion|clearance|discount|outlet|saving)s?\b/i`, tested on the title text and on the heading text, and on the path with every non-letter and non-digit turned into a space (`/region-and-national-specials` gives `region and national specials`, `/special.html` gives `special html`, `/co-sale/` gives `co sale`). **Not a substring test**: "wholesale", "ideal", "dealer", "specialist" and "specialty" must not count. If it does, the verdict is **`unverifiable`** with the new reason **`discounts_not_readable`** (add it to `ValidationReason`; `recordEffect` already maps `unverifiable` to active and URL-verified). If it does not, the verdict stays `ended`, reason `no_discounts`: a plain category page that has no sale words and no discounts is still ended. The words are one constant in `validate.ts`. All four pages above have a sale word in their title, so they become `unverifiable` (Spar and Gelmar become `valid` by the first two rules).
5. **Nothing else changes:** rules 1 to 6 and 9, the challenge, login and error rules, the thresholds and every existing sample's verdict stay as they are. The extractors keep reading only `valid` pages.

### The finalize step (`apps/pipeline/src/repo.ts`, `finalize-run.ts`)

**Do not raise the cap.** `MAX_RUNS_PER_FINALIZE` is 10 for a reason written next to it: D1 allows **50 queries per invocation** and closing a run takes 2 (a count and an update), plus the list: 10 runs is 21 queries, 100 would be 201 and the invocation would fail part way. (The first draft of this story said 100: that was wrong.) The fix is the **order**: `listUnfinishedRuns` becomes `ORDER BY id DESC`, **newest first**, so tonight's run is always among the 10 closed and the older leftovers drain over the next nights. In normal use there is one open run and nothing changes. Update the comment on the constant and on `listUnfinishedRuns` ("Runs with no finish time, newest first"), and the comment of `finalizeRun` if it says oldest. Update the repo and finalize tests that assume the old order (update, do not delete) and add: with 15 open runs one finalize closes the **10 newest** and leaves the 5 oldest open; a second finalize closes those 5; a closed run is not closed again; the number of queries of one finalize with 15 open runs is at most 21 (count them with the fake or the real local database). **Do not touch any run in the live database**: leftover runs 13 to 17 close by themselves, tonight's (17) at the first finalize after the deploy and the rest at the next. ### Gaps and decisions for the developer to note
